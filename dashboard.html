How to Get Your Soil Test Ticket

Get your soil tested in minutes or download your official government report

Option A • Instant Portable Lab Recommended for instant results

SoiLENZ by ArkaShine Innovations

SoiLENZ is a patented, portable AI-enabled backpack soil testing laboratory developed by ArkaShine Innovations Pvt. Ltd. (Bidar & Hubli, Karnataka). It tests 14 key soil parameters (N, P, K, pH, EC, Organic Carbon & micronutrients) in just 10–15 minutes directly on your farmland, printing a thermal receipt with verified chemical metrics.

How to use it:

  1. Collect Soil Sample: Dig 6 inches deep across 4-5 spots in your farm, mix thoroughly and remove surface debris/stones.
  2. Visit Agent / Request Test: Contact an authorized SoiLENZ testing agent or request an on-farm test via ArkaShine Innovations.
  3. Receive Thermal Receipt: Get your printed ticket containing exact N, P, K, pH, EC, OC, Ca, Mg, S, Fe, Mn, Cu, Zn, and B values.
  4. Scan on Seed2Serve: Snap a photo or upload the ticket here. Seed2Serve will auto-fill every value and deliver tailored crop and fertilizer plans!

Option B • Government Scheme

Soil Health Card (SHC) Portal & KVK / RSK

Farmers can also obtain official soil test cards through the Government of India's Soil Health Card scheme. Visit your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), or download your test report directly from the portal using your farmer registration or survey number.
